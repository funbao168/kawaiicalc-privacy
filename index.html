Kawaii Calc Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Kawaii Calc ("the app") is a calculator app for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: it collects nothing.

Information we collect

The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking tools.

Internet access

The app does not request internet permission and works entirely offline. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Data kept on your device

Your calculation history, memory value and key-sound setting are held only in the app's memory on your device while it is running. They are never uploaded.

Permissions

The app does not ask for any special permissions, such as location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage. The key sounds are generated by the app itself.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.
